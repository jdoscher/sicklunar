Coverage = share of one month each point is seen by at least one sensor: sunlit, not behind Earth/Moon, outside Sun/Moon/Earth keep-outs, and bright enough (diffuse sphere, albedo 0.2). Defaults: AMOS 2024 (Bhadauria) for space keep-outs, AMOS 2020 (Dao) for ground (Sun 20°, Moon 5°). Ignores weather, per-site daylight and the Moon's 5° tilt.

Orbits: NASA/JPL Three-Body Periodic Orbits API, propagated in the CR3BP. Not mission-grade.
