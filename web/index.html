    Pick a destination

How it works

Real physics, simplified for learning.

Sicklunar works out each path step by step while Earth and the Moon both pull on your spacecraft. Here is what is real, and what we left out.

Real

  • Paths are computed with Earth’s and the Moon’s gravity pulling at the same time (the three-body problem).
  • The Moon and the Sun are where they really are on the date you pick.
  • Orbits come from NASA/JPL’s catalog of three-body periodic orbits.
  • Launch pads and Moon sites come from cited sources: space agencies, rocket user’s guides and Moon maps from NASA’s Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter.

Simplified

  • The Sun’s pull is left out of the flight paths.
  • Fuel is counted in rounded units of 100 m/s, and the rocket’s climb to orbit isn’t taken from your tank.
  • Numbers differ a little from real missions. When they do, the app says so.
  • It’s for learning, not for planning a real mission.
